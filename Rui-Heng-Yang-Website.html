I am a researcher at Noah's Ark Laboratory working on embodied AI. My current work is on world action models and vision-language-action models for robotic manipulation, with a focus on real-time world action models. Before this, I developed training-free guidance methods for diffusion policies that let a trained policy avoid collisions and replan at inference time. I joined Noah's Ark full time after research internships at Samsung AI Center and Noah's Ark, and I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Computing from the University of Toronto and an Honours B.Sc. in Computer Science from McGill University.
